post.supply · How it works

From a message to a delivered order.

Seven steps, the same every time. You see each one, and nothing is ordered until you approve a price.

We reply by text or WhatsApp.

01 · Steps

Seven steps, in order.

  1. 01Request
  2. 02Specification
  3. 03Sourcing
  4. 04Comparison
  5. 05Approval
  6. 06Delivery
  7. 07Reconciliation
  1. 01

    Request

    You send what you need in any format, on the channel you already use. A photo of a list is enough to start.

  2. 02

    Specification

    We turn the request into a written spec: product, grade, dimensions, quantity, destination and need-by date. If something is missing, we ask once, clearly.

  3. 03

    Sourcing

    We take the spec to manufacturers and stockists that can produce or hold it.

  4. 04

    Comparison

    Offers come back in different formats. We normalize them so they compare like for like, and note what we have verified about each supplier and what we have not.

  5. 05

    Approval

    You receive one delivered price per option, in writing, with its validity window. Nothing is ordered until you approve.

  6. 06

    Delivery

    We coordinate production, inspection where agreed, freight and documents. Updates arrive on the channel you used to ask.

  7. 07

    Reconciliation

    After delivery, we compare the final landed cost with the quote you approved, line by line, and show you the result.

03 · Limits

What we will not do.

  • Publish your request

    Your request is not listed anywhere public.

  • Add fees to your approved price

    The price you approve is the price you pay for the material delivered.

  • Promise savings we cannot show

    We compare against your own invoices and let the numbers speak.

  • Present a plan as a feature

    If it is not live, it carries a status that says so.

04 · Questions

Questions about the process.

How long does a quote take?

It depends on the material, the quantity and how complete the request is. When we confirm your specification, we tell you when to expect the quote, and we update you if that changes.

Who are the suppliers?

Manufacturers and stockists that can meet the specification. Each offer states what we have verified about the supplier and what we have not, so you can weigh it yourself.

What is BuildFlow?

BuildFlow is the post.supply text-to-quote feature. It reads a request written in plain language and drafts a structured specification for our desk to confirm. It is a prototype today, and a person checks every quote before you see it.

Can I change the order after I approve?

Tell us as soon as possible. Whether a change is possible, and what it costs, depends on how far production and freight have gone. We confirm any change in writing before acting on it.
